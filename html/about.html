Brewing Happiness Since Day One

Sun Tea & Coffee started with a simple dream: to make a space that would warm, refresh and truly bring happiness with each cup. With the spirit of the sun and the warmth of the camaraderie of a drink, we felt compelled to promote beverages prepared using the finest of tea leaves, top-notch coffee beans, and fresh fruits.

Each recipe is carefully designed to create the ideal balance of taste, quality and consistency. From a refreshing fruit tea to a smooth milk tea and a rich cup of coffee, each coffee is made with care to make moments that you can share with family, friends and love.

Today, Sun Tea & Coffee is continuing to grow, and we continue to maintain our original values. We continue to innovate, provide outstanding service and make unforgettable memories, cup by cup.
