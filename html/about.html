Brewing Calm Since Day One

Moon Tea & Coffee started with a simple dream: to create a cosy space that brings calm, comfort, and happiness with every cup. Inspired by the peaceful glow of moonlight, we craft beverages using premium tea leaves, rich coffee beans, and fresh fruits.

Each recipe is designed to deliver the perfect balance of flavour, quality, and consistency. From soothing fruit teas to creamy milk teas and aromatic coffees, every drink is made with care to create memorable moments shared with family, friends, and loved ones.

Today, Moon Tea & Coffee continues to grow while staying true to our original values. We innovate, serve with passion, and create unforgettable experiences—cup by cup.
